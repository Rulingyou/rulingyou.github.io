About

Meet your teacher

Hi, I'm Rulin. I run an independent piano studio where every student gets individual attention, a clear plan, and music they're excited to practice.

My teaching blends a strong classical foundation (technique, reading, theory, and ear training) with the freedom to explore pop, film, and the music students love. Lessons are paced to each person: young children learn through games and rhythm, teens work toward goals like exams and recitals, and adults learn at a comfortable, pressure-free pace.

Students perform in studio recitals and can prepare for graded examinations and competitions.

FAQ

Common questions

What age can my child start?

Most children are ready around age 5–6, when they can recognize numbers and letters and focus for about 20–30 minutes. A trial lesson is a great way to find out.

Do I need a real piano?

An acoustic piano is ideal, but a digital piano or keyboard with 88 full-size, weighted keys and a sustain pedal works well, especially for beginners.

How much should students practice?

Consistency matters more than length. Young beginners do well with 10–15 minutes a day; older students typically practice 30–60 minutes.

Are there recitals?

Yes. The studio holds recitals so students can share what they've learned in a supportive setting. Participation is encouraged but never required.

I'm an adult with no experience. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Adult beginners are very welcome, and lessons are designed around your goals, pace, and schedule.
